# Phase 3H counterfactual answer-sensitivity preflight

This zero-network preflight responds to Phase 3G's answer-grounding diagnosis. It freezes 40 wholly new 2WikiMultiHopQA comparison tasks, split evenly across attribute and bridge-attribute comparison. Every admitted task has two distinct source-verifiable answers: the dataset answer under the question's comparison direction and the other compared entity under the preregistered inverse procedure.

The proposed schedule contains 200 unique requests across cold, contextual, forced incompatible-control, and two reversed-order dual conditions. Private gold records both answers and both evidence traces; neither answer is included in any canonical request. All prior task, logical-call, and request-hash overlaps are zero.

The primary gate is answer grounding, not selector uptake: after the target and inverse procedures are executed and the observable operation changes, final answers must flip to their corresponding preregistered answers. The proposal remains `not_authorized`; no network, provider, model, paid, later-stage, cross-domain, or formal-scaling call was made.
